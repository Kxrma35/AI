Cookie Policy

Last updated:

JOESTAR does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It uses a small amount of strictly necessary browser storage.

What is stored

Name / typePurposeCategoryLifetime
Firebase Authentication session (IndexedDB / local storage, set by Google’s Firebase SDK)Keeps you signed inStrictly necessaryUntil you sign out or clear site data
joestar_consent_v1 (local storage)Remembers your answer to the cookie noticeStrictly necessaryUntil you clear site data

Because this storage is strictly necessary for the service you request, it does not need your consent under most cookie laws, but we tell you about it. If we add optional storage in future, it will be off by default until you opt in, and you will be able to change your mind at any time.

Managing storage

You can at any time, or clear site data in your browser settings (this will sign you out).

Third-party requests

Fonts, icons and the 3D graphics library are served from this website, not from external CDNs. The sign-in screens load Google’s Firebase SDK from gstatic.com, and signing in with Google contacts Google. See the Privacy Policy.
