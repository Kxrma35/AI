3. Voice input

The microphone button uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. In Chrome and some other browsers, the audio is sent to the browser vendor’s servers (for Chrome, Google) to be transcribed. JOESTAR itself only receives the resulting text and never receives or stores the audio. The microphone is only used when you press the button. You can type instead.

4. Shared AI memory (important)

JOESTAR stores your conversations so it can recall them later. Each stored conversation is tagged with your account ID, and your history and memory are only shown to you. Conversations stored before account tagging was introduced are not attached to any account.

5. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis: we process account and conversation data to provide the service you asked for (contract); we process technical logs for security and reliability (legitimate interests); and we rely on your consent for optional storage, if any is ever introduced, and for the age and terms confirmation you give when creating an account.

6. Retention

  • Account and conversation data are kept until you delete them or ask us to.
  • Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for its standard period.
  • Third parties keep data under their own policies (for example AI providers may retain requests for a limited time for abuse monitoring).

7. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

  • Delete your conversations or delete your account and all data directly in the app: sign in, then use Privacy in the top bar.
  • For access, correction, a copy of your data, or if you cannot sign in, email . We aim to respond within 30 days.

8. Children

JOESTAR is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect children’s data. Creating an account requires confirming that you are at least 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

The providers listed above may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer (such as standard contractual clauses).

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS, access to conversations requires a verified sign-in, and secrets are stored outside the public website files. No system is perfectly secure; tell us promptly if you find a problem.

11. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date above. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
